Block Checken Shooter

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Block Checken Shooter (the “Game,” “we,” or “us”) processes information when you play the Game. The Game is an online, real-time multiplayer game for up to eight players per match, available for Android and Windows. We use services supplied by Unity Technologies to make online multiplayer play possible.

1. Information used to provide the Game

The Game does not ask you to register with an email address or password. However, online multiplayer functionality requires technical information to be processed, including:

  • Anonymous player identifier: Unity Authentication automatically assigns a player ID and uses authentication/session tokens to recognize your game session. “Anonymous” means no email/password is required; it does not mean that no identifier is processed.
  • Network information: IP addresses and connection information are processed by Unity Multiplayer Sessions / Lobby and Unity Relay to enable connectivity, lobby discovery, and online play.
  • Multiplayer and gameplay information: session or lobby membership and game-state information needed to synchronize a multiplayer match may be transmitted between connected players and Unity services.
  • In-game text messages: if you use the text-chat feature, the messages you enter are transmitted to other players participating in the multiplayer session. The Game uses a bad-word filter, but no automated filter can prevent all inappropriate messages.

Please do not share your real name, address, phone number, email address, passwords, or other personal information in chat.

2. How information is used

Information is processed for anonymous sign-in, lobby discovery, matchmaking or joining games, connecting players, transmitting chat and gameplay events, maintaining multiplayer sessions, and ensuring the availability and security of online play.

3. Services and other players

The Game uses Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies, specifically Unity Authentication, Unity Multiplayer Sessions / Lobby, and Unity Relay. Netcode for GameObjects handles the game's networking. Unity processes technical data on our behalf to provide these services. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for additional details about Unity's practices.

During multiplayer play, players in the same session receive game-state data and any messages you choose to send in chat. Unity Relay routes network traffic so players generally do not need to connect directly to each other's IP addresses.

We do not use player information for targeted advertising or sell player personal information. We do not currently integrate an advertising, analytics, or separate social-login service into the Game.

4. Data retention

Some technical information is retained by Unity services as necessary to provide multiplayer features. According to Unity's service documentation, Unity Relay retains personal data for approximately 30 days; Unity Lobby may retain player identifiers in logs for up to 30 days. Unity Authentication player records and credentials may remain until deleted through the appropriate Unity account/developer deletion process. Gameplay state and chat messages are transmitted for live multiplayer functionality; retention of any copies in host devices, application logs, or other systems depends on how the Game is configured.

We do not operate a dedicated game server or intentionally maintain an independent central archive of player chat. If you request deletion, we will review information associated with your player identifier and take appropriate steps to request or perform deletion through relevant Unity services, subject to applicable retention requirements.

5. Children's privacy and online interaction

We aim to make the Game safe for players, including younger players where permitted. The Game includes multiplayer text chat. Players should not share personal information, and parents or guardians should supervise children using online multiplayer features. Chat filtering reduces certain inappropriate words but is not a guarantee of safety.

If you are a parent or guardian concerned about information relating to a child, contact us using the email address below. Any additional age-based safeguards or parental-consent mechanisms required by applicable law must be provided before offering applicable features to children.

6. Security

We use established multiplayer infrastructure from Unity to support network sessions. No internet service or content-filtering system can guarantee absolute security. Please avoid putting personal information into chat messages.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You may stop using the Game at any time. You can contact us to ask about information associated with your anonymous player ID, to request access or deletion where applicable, or to report a privacy concern. We may need your in-game player ID or other information sufficient to locate the relevant record; please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.
